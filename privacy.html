Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with The Courts, we may collect information such as:

  • Parent or guardian name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Athlete name, age, grade, and sport
  • Membership and registration information
  • Program participation history
  • Purchase and transaction information
  • Communications and inquiries
  • Information you choose to provide through forms
  • Photos or video where collected or used with appropriate permission
  • Website and device information, such as browser type, IP address, cookies, and analytics information

Payment information may be processed directly by third-party payment providers. The Courts may not directly receive or store complete payment card information.

How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Manage memberships, registrations, bookings, and purchases
  • Communicate about schedules, programs, events, facility updates, and account activity
  • Provide customer and member support
  • Help families identify appropriate programs or coaches
  • Process transactions
  • Operate and improve our programs, facility, website, and customer experience
  • Send news or marketing communications where permitted
  • Support safety and facility operations
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, or security issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

Athletes + Children

The Courts serves youth athletes, but our website, registration, purchasing, and account features are intended to be used by parents, guardians, and other adults rather than independently by children under 13.

Parents and guardians may provide information about their children when:

  • Registering for programs
  • Creating or managing athlete profiles
  • Purchasing memberships
  • Communicating with The Courts

Children under 13 should not independently submit personal information through our website.

If we learn that personal information has been collected directly online from a child under 13 in a manner that requires parental consent, we will take appropriate steps consistent with applicable law.

Parents or guardians who believe a child has submitted personal information directly to The Courts may contact hello@playthecourts.com.

Cookies + Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, understand how visitors use it, improve performance, and support communications or marketing where applicable.

The specific cookies and technologies used may change as our website and services evolve.

You may be able to control certain cookies through your browser settings or any cookie-management tools made available on the site.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party service providers to support functions such as:

  • Memberships and program registration
  • Scheduling and account management
  • Payment processing
  • Website hosting
  • Email and communications
  • Analytics
  • Forms
  • Merchandise and fulfillment
  • Maps and other website features

These providers may process information as necessary to provide services to The Courts or to you.

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

Marketing Communications

If you sign up to receive updates from The Courts, we may send information about programs, camps, basketball leagues, events, merchandise, and other Courts news.

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the unsubscribe link included in those messages.

You may still receive operational or transactional communications related to memberships, bookings, registrations, purchases, schedules, facility updates, safety, or account activity.

When We Share Information

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information with service providers that help us:

  • Operate The Courts
  • Manage memberships or registrations
  • Process transactions
  • Communicate with families
  • Maintain our website
  • Fulfill purchases
  • Provide other business services

We may also disclose information:

  • When required by law
  • When reasonably necessary to protect safety, rights, or property
  • In connection with a business transaction involving The Courts

Data Security + Retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information.

No website, system, or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for:

  • The purposes for which it was collected
  • Legitimate business and operational needs
  • Safety and recordkeeping
  • Legal, accounting, or reporting requirements

Your Choices

You may contact us to:

  • Update or correct information
  • Ask about information we maintain
  • Request deletion where appropriate
  • Change communication preferences
  • Ask a privacy-related question

Certain information may need to be retained for legal, safety, accounting, fraud-prevention, or operational purposes.

Depending on applicable law and your circumstances, additional privacy rights may apply.

Contact hello@playthecourts.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
