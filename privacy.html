Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 21, 2026

The Courts, LLC (“The Courts,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website, contact us, register for programming, purchase products, or otherwise interact with The Courts.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect information including:

  • Parent or guardian name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Billing and transaction information
  • Athlete name, age, grade, and sport
  • Program registrations and participation history
  • Membership information
  • Communications and inquiries
  • Information you voluntarily provide through forms
  • Website usage and device information, such as browser type, IP address, cookies, and analytics data

Payment information may be processed directly by third-party payment providers. The Courts may not directly store complete payment card information.

How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Manage memberships, registrations, and purchases
  • Communicate with families about schedules, programs, events, and facility updates
  • Provide customer and member support
  • Match athletes with appropriate programs or coaches
  • Process payments and transactions
  • Improve our programs, website, and member experience
  • Send news and marketing communications where permitted
  • Maintain safety and facility operations
  • Prevent fraud or misuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

Information About Athletes and Children

The Courts serves youth athletes, but our website, registration, purchasing, and account features are intended to be used by parents, guardians, and other adults, not independently by children under 13.

Parents and guardians may provide information about their children when registering for programs or communicating with The Courts.

Children under 13 should not independently submit personal information through our website.

If we learn that personal information has been collected directly online from a child under 13 in a manner requiring parental consent, we will take appropriate steps consistent with applicable law.

Parents or guardians who believe a child has submitted personal information directly to us may contact us at hello@playthecourts.com.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party providers to support our operations, including services for:

  • Membership and program registration
  • Scheduling
  • Payment processing
  • Website hosting
  • E-commerce and merchandise
  • Email communications
  • Analytics
  • Forms and customer communications

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

Note for final version: specifically identify Upper Hand, Fourthwall, our payment processor, analytics provider, email platform, and any other material services once the final tech stack is confirmed.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, understand how visitors use it, improve performance, and support marketing.

You may be able to control certain cookies through your browser or available cookie-management tools.

Marketing Communications

If you sign up to receive updates from The Courts, we may send you information about programs, camps, clinics, leagues, events, merchandise, and other Courts news.

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link included in those messages.

You may still receive operational communications related to memberships, purchases, registrations, schedules, safety, or other transactions.

Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information with service providers that help us operate The Courts, process transactions, deliver programs, maintain our website, or communicate with families.

We may also disclose information where required by law, necessary to protect safety or legal rights, or as part of a business transaction involving The Courts.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information.

No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, our legitimate business needs, and applicable legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.

Your Choices

You may contact us to:

  • Update or correct information
  • Request information about data we maintain
  • Ask us to delete information where appropriate
  • Change your communication preferences
  • Ask a privacy-related question

Certain information may need to be retained for legal, safety, accounting, or operational purposes.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
