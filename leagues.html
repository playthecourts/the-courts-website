Real competition, real teammates, and real development through seasonal basketball and volleyball leagues for youth and high school athletes.

Basketball · Fall League

The Courts Fall Basketball League

Register your athlete for the upcoming fall season with West Nashville Sports League (WNSL). Athletes will practice weekly and compete in Saturday games throughout the season.

SeasonOctober 24 – December 13
Regular SeasonOctober 24 – December 5
End of Season TournamentDecember 11 – 13
Register Your Athlete → View Game Days →
How It Works

From registration to game day.

01 · Register Your Athlete

Families register individual athletes for the season. Space is limited by age group and roster availability.

02 · Evaluation + Team Placement

Athletes will attend a player evaluation in mid-September so coaches can assess skill levels and make thoughtful team placements.

03 · Practice + Develop

Weekly practices focus on skill development, team play, and preparing athletes for competition.

04 · Compete

Teams compete in Saturday games throughout the season, culminating in the WNSL End of Season Tournament.

Player Evaluations · Mid-September

Before teams are finalized, athletes will participate in an evaluation to help coaches build balanced, competitive teams where every athlete has the opportunity to develop and contribute.

Evaluation Fee: $25

The evaluation fee will be applied toward fall registration for athletes who join a team. Evaluation date and details will be shared with registered families.

Ready to Play?

Register your athlete to get started. We'll follow up with evaluation details, pricing, practice schedules, and everything you need for the season.
