Before We Pick Teams

Come Show Us Your Game.

Players will attend an evaluation before teams are finalized. We're looking at skill level, experience, how athletes move, make decisions, compete, and fit into a team.

No need to audition for the NBA. Just come play.

Player Evaluations · Mid-September

This is for team placement, not a tryout — your athlete doesn't need to "prepare" for it, and it isn't meant to be intimidating.

Evaluation Fee: $25

The $25 fee will be applied toward fall registration for athletes who join a team. Evaluation date and details will be shared with registered families.

Why League Ball?

Because Eventually You Have to Play.

Training matters. But athletes also need teammates, decisions, mistakes, pressure, wins, losses, and the occasional possession where absolutely nothing goes according to plan. That's basketball too.

Teammates.

Learn how to play with people, not just next to them.

Decisions.

The game doesn't pause while you figure it out.

Competition.

Want to win. Play hard. Learn what to do either way.
