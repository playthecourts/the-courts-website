How Do We Build the Divisions?

We start with grade and gender, then use registrations and evaluations to create groupings that make sense for the athletes who actually show up. The goal isn’t the biggest possible roster — it’s balanced teams, smaller groups, meaningful playing time, and a good competitive fit. Final divisions may combine adjacent grades depending on registration.

Fall League Evaluations

Raise Your Hand. We’ll See You at Evaluations.

We’re building teams for the upcoming fall basketball season with West Nashville Sports League (WNSL), and the first step is Evaluation Day.

Saturday, September 12 · 3 PM

There are no cuts. Evaluations simply help us get to know each athlete, understand skill levels, and build balanced teams with smaller rosters, meaningful playing time, and the right level of competition.

Evaluation Fee: $25We’ll send you the evaluation registration and payment link shortly after you submit this form. If your athlete joins a fall team, the full $25 evaluation fee will be applied toward Fall League registration.

What Happens Next

  1. 1
    Raise your hand here.

    Complete evaluation registration.

  3. 3

    We’ll use evaluations to help create thoughtful, balanced teams. There are no cuts.

    Hear from us after evaluations.

    Following Evaluation Day, we’ll update you on your athlete’s team and send you the link to complete Fall League registration.
