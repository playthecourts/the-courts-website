Why League Ball?

Because Eventually, Somebody Has to Take the Shot.

Training gives athletes the reps. Games give them the decisions. Teammates. Pressure. Mistakes. Big plays. Weird plays. Possessions where absolutely nothing goes according to plan. That's basketball, too.

Teammates.

Learn to play with people, not just next to them.

Decisions.

The game doesn't pause while you figure it out.

Compete.

Want to win. Play hard. Learn either way.
