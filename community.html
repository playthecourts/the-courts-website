We Play Here. We Give Back Here.

Nolensville is our home court.

Being part of a community means showing up for it.

As The Courts grows, so will the ways we give back. We want to create opportunities for our athletes and families to serve together, support local schools and organizations, and help more kids get in the game.

Volunteer days. School partnerships. Community projects. Athlete access. And good ideas we haven't thought of yet.

The better The Courts does, the more good we should be able to do.
