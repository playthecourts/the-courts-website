Community

More Than
the Game.

We're here to help kids become better athletes.
And better teammates, leaders, and humans.
Because how you show up off the court matters, too.

What We Care About

Work hard. Be a great teammate. Show up for others.

Sports give kids a chance to practice things that matter long after the game is over.

Work Hard.

Put in the reps. Keep going.

Be a Great Teammate.

Make the people around you better.

Show Respect.

For your team, your coaches, your family, and the game.

Have Character.

Do the right thing when no one is keeping score.

Give Back.

If you're part of a community, show up for it.

We Play Here. We Give Back Here.

Nolensville is our home court.

Being part of a community means showing up for it.

As The Courts grows, so will the ways we give back. We want to create opportunities for our athletes and families to serve together, support local schools and organizations, and help more kids get in the game.

Volunteer days. School partnerships. Community projects. Athlete access. And good ideas we haven't thought of yet.

The better The Courts does, the more good we should be able to do.
