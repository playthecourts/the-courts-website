The Courts Community

We're here to build more than athletes.

Of course we want kids to get better.

But sports can teach so much more than how to shoot, serve, or compete. They teach you to work for something. To be a teammate. To handle hard things. To show up for other people.

At The Courts, who you're becoming matters just as much as how you're playing.

Better athletes. Great teammates. Good humans.

We want The Courts to be a place where kids are challenged, supported, and known.

A place where they learn to compete hard and still pick up a teammate. To take coaching. To keep going when something is difficult. To celebrate someone else's win. And to understand that being part of a team means showing up for more than yourself.

Those lessons last a lot longer than any season.

Work Hard

Put in the reps. Keep going.

Be a Great Teammate

Make the people around you better.

Show Respect

For your team, your coaches, your family, and the game.

Have Character

Do the right thing when no one is keeping score.

Give Back

If you're part of a community, show up for it.

We Play Here. We Give Back Here.

Our community is part of our team.

Nolensville is our community, too. We want The Courts to be the kind of local business that doesn't just operate here, but shows up here.

As we grow, that means creating opportunities for Courts families to serve together, partnering with local schools and organizations, supporting community needs, and helping more kids get in the game.

Serve Together.

Volunteer days, donation drives, and projects athletes and families can do side by side.

Show Up Locally.

Partner with schools, PTOs, nonprofits, youth organizations, and community initiatives.

Create Access.

Build toward scholarships and other opportunities that help more kids play.

The better The Courts does, the more good we should be able to do.

Good ideas don't have to come from us.

We want to hear from schools, PTOs, nonprofits, local businesses, youth organizations, coaches, and community groups that want to create something meaningful together.

Maybe it's a clinic. A fundraiser. A volunteer day. A scholarship. A community event. A program partnership. Or something we haven't thought of yet.

If it's good for our kids and good for our community, we're listening.
