Our Story

Hello, Nolensville.

We're opening October 1, and we can't wait to meet you.

The Courts was built around a simple idea: young athletes deserve great coaching, room to grow, and a place they genuinely want to be.

We're proud to be part of the Nolensville community and excited for what's ahead, from the athletes who train with us to the families cheering them on.

We'll see you on the court.

How We Play

Serious about development. Not serious about everything.

We care about quality coaching, thoughtful programming, and creating opportunities for athletes to improve. But we also believe sports are supposed to be fun.

Whether someone is working toward the next level, learning a new sport, or stepping onto the court for the first time, they should feel welcome here.

  • Quality coaching and instruction
  • Programming for different ages, interests, and skill levels
  • A positive, competitive environment
  • More opportunities to play throughout the year
  • A facility that feels welcoming to athletes and families
