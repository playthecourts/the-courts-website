Our Story

Hello, ✦ ✧ ✦ ✧ ✦ Opening October 1Nolensville.

We open October 1. Shoes tied. Let's go.

The Courts is built around what we think makes sports great: good coaches, athletes who want to work, teammates who make each other better,
and people who show up for one another. That's the kind of community we want on our courts.

See you on the court.
Melissa Carle
Founder, The Courts

How We Play

Serious About Development.
Not Everything Else.

We care about good coaching, meaningful reps, and learning how to compete without making every Tuesday feel like Game 7.

Do the Work.

No magic drill. No shortcut.
Show up. Put in the reps.

Be Coachable.

Listen. Ask questions. Try it differently.
Nobody has it all figured out.

Compete Well.

Play hard. Want to win.
Know how to handle it either way.

Be a Great Teammate.

Bring energy. Pick people up.
Make the gym better when you're in it.

Coaching can be a weirdly solo job.

You're building the workouts, texting the parents, chasing gym space, managing schedules, answering "what time again?" for the fifth time, and somehow still expected to actually coach.

That's a lot
of tabs open.

Coach Collective is a home base for independent basketball and volleyball coaches to train their athletes, grow their programs, and have other good coaches around while they do it.
