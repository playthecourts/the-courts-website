Hello, Opening October 1Nolensville.

I had the opportunity to experience NextGen first as a parent, which gave me a front-row seat to what made this place special.

I watched my son train, play, make friends, and find a place he genuinely wanted to keep coming back to. I saw coaches who cared, relationships that formed, and a community that grew around the court.

I was a parent here before I was the owner.

I grew up as a competitive cheerleader, so I've spent a lot of my life on the sidelines cheering other people on.

Turns out, I still am.

I know how much it matters to have someone in your corner. Someone who believes in you, pushes you, and keeps cheering through the great games, the hard ones, and everything in between.

I'll be cheering for your kid, too.

Not just for the points they score, but for the teammate they're becoming. The confidence they're building. The way they listen, help someone up, try again, and keep going when it's hard.

Getting better at the game matters. Who they become along the way matters, too.

The Courts is our chance to build on what families already loved about NextGen and keep creating an even stronger experience for athletes on the court and families on the sidelines.

I'm grateful for the opportunity to carry that forward, and really excited about what we get to build together from here.

And I promise I'll work just as hard on this place as I'm asking your kid to work when they walk through the orange door.

Melissa Carle

How We Play

Serious About Development.
Not Everything Else.

We care about good coaching, meaningful reps, and learning how to compete without making every Tuesday feel like Game 7.

01

Do the Work.

No magic drill. No shortcut.
Show up. Put in the reps.

02

Be Coachable.

Listen. Ask questions. Try it differently.
Nobody has it all figured out.

03

Compete Well.

Play hard. Want to win.
Know how to handle it either way.

04

Be a Great Teammate.

Bring energy. Pick people up.
Make the gym better when you're in it.

Community

Nolensville is Our Home Court.

Being part of a community means more than opening the doors. It means showing up.

We want The Courts to give back to the schools, organizations, families, and causes that make Nolensville such a great place to raise kids.

And we want our athletes to have opportunities to
be part of that, too. Because being a great teammate shouldn't stop at the gym door.

School partnerships. Volunteer projects. Community service. A chance to show up for the place that shows up for us.
