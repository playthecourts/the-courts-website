About

Hello, Opening October 1Nolensville.

I was a parent here before I was the owner.

I spent a lot of time at NextGen on the sidelines, watching my son train, play, make friends, and find a place he genuinely wanted to keep coming back to.

I saw what was already special here. And, like most parents, I had a few thoughts about what could be even better.

Sports have always been part of my life. I played basketball growing up, competed in cheer, and spent a lot of time on the sidelines cheering other people on.

Turns out, I'm still doing it.

I know how much it matters to have someone in your corner. Someone who believes in you, wants you to get better, and keeps cheering through the great games, the hard ones, and everything in between.

On the court and off.

I'll be cheering for your kid, too.

Not just for the points they put on the board, but for the teammate they're becoming. The confidence they're building. The way they help someone up, listen to a coach, try again, and keep going when something doesn't come easily.

Because resilience matters. Character matters. Feeling like you belong matters.

Of course I want great coaching here. More reps. More opportunities to train, play, compete, and get better.

But I want kids to become better here, too.

The Courts is our chance to build on what families already loved about NextGen and make the experience even better, for the athletes on the court and the families on the sidelines.

Great coaches. More opportunities. Clear communication. A place kids want to come back to and parents feel good about bringing them.

And we're not going to get everything right on day one.

But with your support, your ideas, and your feedback, we'll keep making it better.

I have plenty of opinions too. You're in good company.

If there's something you love, something we could do better, or something you wish existed here, tell me.

You can always find me at melissa@playthecourts.com.

I promise I'm going to work just as hard on this place as I'm asking your kid to work when they walk through the orange door.

See you on the court.
Melissa Carle
Founder, The Courts

How We Play

Serious About Development.
Not Everything Else.

We care about good coaching, meaningful reps, and learning how to compete without making every Tuesday feel like Game 7.

Do the Work.

No magic drill. No shortcut.
Show up. Put in the reps.

Be Coachable.

Listen. Ask questions. Try it differently.
Nobody has it all figured out.

Compete Well.

Play hard. Want to win.
Know how to handle it either way.

Be a Great Teammate.

Bring energy. Pick people up.
Make the gym better when you're in it.

More Than the Scoreboard

The Courts Athlete of the Month.

This one isn't about who scored the most points.

Each month, we'll recognize a basketball or volleyball athlete who shows us what being part of The Courts is really about.

The teammate who picks someone up.

The athlete who keeps working when it gets hard.

The kid who listens, encourages, includes, competes well, and makes the gym better just by being in it.

The stuff that might not show up on the scoreboard. We see it.

Our First One Is Coming October 24.

We'll announce the first Courts Athlete of the Month at our Grand Opening Celebration.

The Courts T-Shirt Because obviously.
Free Pass for a Friend Because good teammates bring people with them.

Bragging rights for being the kind of athlete we want more of around here.

Community

Nolensville is Our Home Court.

Being part of a community means more than opening the doors. It means showing up.

We want The Courts to give back to the schools, organizations, families, and causes that make Nolensville such a great place to raise kids.

And we want our athletes to have opportunities to be part of that, too. Because being a great teammate shouldn't stop at the gym door.

School partnerships. Volunteer projects. Community service. A chance to show up for the place that shows up for us.

Our First Chance to Give Back Is Coming in December.

We'll organize a Courts community give-back opportunity once a quarter, giving our athletes and families a chance to show up together for Nolensville.
