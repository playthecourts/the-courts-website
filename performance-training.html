Performance Training

Sport-specific strength, speed, agility, and injury prevention — built for the demands of basketball and volleyball, not generic gym work. Athletes train to move better, not just lift more.

Overview

Train the athlete, not just the sport.

Basketball and volleyball demand explosive first steps, real deceleration control, and bodies that hold up over a long season. Our performance program builds that foundation with age-appropriate strength work, movement mechanics, and injury-prevention training.

Small-group sessions mean real coaching attention — not a crowded weight room. Every session is designed to make athletes faster, stronger, and more durable on the court, not just in the gym.

Pairs naturally with basketball or volleyball membership, or stands alone for athletes focused purely on athletic development.

What athletes actually gain.

Strength & Power

Training Tracks

Programming built for where an athlete is now.

Introductory strength training paired with speed and agility work as bodies grow.

Ages 15–18

Higher-intensity strength and conditioning built around in-season and off-season demands.

All Ages

Injury Prevention Track

Low-impact mobility and prehab work for athletes returning from or avoiding injury.
