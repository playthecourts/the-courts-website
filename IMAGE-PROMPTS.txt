HOUSE STYLE — paste once, as a standing instruction
================================================================================

Style: candid documentary youth-sports photography. 35mm full-frame look,
natural shallow depth of field, a little real motion blur where there is
movement. Warm indoor gym light from overhead fixtures, light maple hardwood
bouncing soft amber onto skin and walls. Muted filmic color with deep true
blacks — not bright, not saturated, not HDR. A single warm orange accent may
appear in apparel, cones, or equipment; nothing else is colorful.

Setting: a clean, modern, private training facility. Light maple hardwood,
matte black walls and structure, adjustable hoops, a volleyball net system.
Lived-in but tidy. Completely blank walls — no signage, no banners, no
scoreboards, no championship boards.

People: youth athletes, roughly ages 8 to 16, mixed genders and mixed builds,
in plain unbranded athletic wear. Nobody looks at the camera. Nobody poses or
smiles for the lens. Expressions are working expressions — focus, effort, a
real laugh between reps.

Framing: imperfect and observed, as if the photographer was already in the gym.
Slightly off-center, occasional foreground blur, natural crops.

Rendering: photorealistic, sharp, high detail.

Do not include: logos, brand marks, wordmarks, text, numbers, signage,
watermarks, jersey names, school names, spectators or bleacher crowds,
trophies, confetti, adults playing, distorted hands or fingers, extra limbs,
fused or duplicated balls, illustration or CGI look, heavy vignette, lens
flare, glossy over-processing.
