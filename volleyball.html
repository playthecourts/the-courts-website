Volleyball

Read the Game. Play Together.

Volleyball rewards players who never stop moving their feet.
Get the technique right. Get a lot of reps.
Learn to read your teammates.
Then see if it holds up when the ball isn't coming right to you.

How We Train

Learn It. Trust It. Use It.

Anybody can pass a ball that's served right at them. We build the technique, rep the footwork, speed up the tempo, and put players in situations where the ball doesn't come easy. Because in a real rally, nothing arrives the way you drew it up.

01

Pass

Every point starts with a touch nobody remembers. Get under the ball, set your platform, and give your setter something to work with.

Make the next touch easier, not more interesting.

AGAIN.
02

Set

A good set is invisible — the hitter never has to adjust. Soft hands, quick feet, and a target picked before the ball even arrives.

No half-sends.

03

Attack

Footwork first, then the arm. Learn to hit around a block, not just through one. Find the gap and go get it.

A little less "good luck." A little more "go get it."

04

Compete

Call the ball. Cover your hitter. Play the next one like it's the only one. This is where practice either shows up or it doesn't.

If it hasn't hit the floor, we're still playing.

The Work Changes as They Do.

A 7-year-old and a 15-year-old shouldn't be doing the same workout. The fundamentals stay. The speed, pressure, and expectations grow with the player.

Ages 6–9

Build the Base.

Ball control. Footwork. Learning to track the ball off a serve. Lots of touches.

Learn the game. Love playing it. Ask for one more rally.

Ages 10–13

Add Speed. Add Decisions.

Cleaner technique. Faster footwork. Real positional roles start to matter.

Less thinking about the touch. More knowing where to put it.

Ages 14+

Make It Game Ready.

Sharper reps. Smarter reads. More pressure. More purpose.

The small habits are the whole game now.
