Membership Policies

The basics of being a Courts member.

This page is the canonical source for Courts membership policies. For plan pricing and what's included with each tier, see Membership.

Monthly Billing + Renewal

Memberships bill monthly and renew automatically on the same billing date each month until canceled in accordance with this policy.

To confirm: exact billing date logic (e.g. join-date anniversary vs. 1st of month) and how partial first months are handled.

Cancellations

Memberships are month-to-month with no long-term contract. To cancel, give us at least 30 days' notice through your member account or by contacting us.

To confirm: how the 30-day notice period lines up with the next billing date, exactly how cancellation requests are submitted, and when cancellation formally takes effect.

Membership Changes

You can move to a different membership tier as your athlete's needs change — training more, adding a second athlete, or scaling back. Reach out and we'll help you find the plan that fits.

Pauses / Freezes

To confirm: whether membership pauses or freezes (e.g. for injury, travel, or off-season) are offered, and if so, the process, notice, and any limits.

Failed Payments

If a membership payment fails, we'll reach out to update your payment method. Continued access to unlimited or included programming may depend on keeping your account current.

To confirm: retry schedule, grace period, and what happens to registrations if payment isn't resolved.

What's Included

Included benefits vary by membership — group training frequency, private training and Dr. Dish pricing, and priority registration all differ by plan. See Membership for current plan details.

Scheduled Access, Not Unrestricted Access

Membership provides access to eligible programming during published, scheduled sessions. It does not mean an athlete can arrive at the facility whenever it's open and use the court. All programming remains subject to the published schedule, registration, age and sport eligibility, capacity, and facility availability.

Class Registration + Capacity

Group training sessions have limited capacity. Registering in advance helps guarantee your athlete's spot.

Late Cancellations / No-Shows

To confirm: whether a late-cancellation or no-show policy applies to registered sessions, and if so, the notice window and any consequences.

Refunds + Credits

Unless required by law, membership fees are generally nonrefundable once billed.

Business decision: finalize refund/credit policy for billing errors, facility closures, or other exceptions.

Changes to Programming

Schedules, coaches, capacity, and programming may change. The Courts will make reasonable efforts to communicate significant changes to affected members.
