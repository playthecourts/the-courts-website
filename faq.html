The basics on training, membership, scheduling, and getting started at The Courts.

Getting Started

We work with youth athletes through high school across basketball and volleyball. Programs vary by age, sport, and experience level.

You’re also welcome to drop off and return at the end of the session. If you do, please be back on time for pickup. Respecting each other’s time is part of The Courts standard. Membership

It depends on the plan.

Weekly includes four eligible Group Training sessions per monthly billing cycle.

Unlimited includes unlimited eligible Group Training and Open Gym.

Full Court adds one Private Training session and two Dr. Dish sessions each month.

Family Unlimited includes Unlimited benefits for two athletes, with additional athletes available for $100/month each.

Memberships are month-to-month and may be canceled with 30 days’ notice.

We don’t routinely pause or freeze memberships, but we’re happy to talk through extended injuries or unusual circumstances.

View Membership Plans →

Billing + Cancellations

Memberships are billed monthly based on your billing date. Camps, leagues, training, and other programs are charged when you register or book.

Contact us at least 30 days before you’d like your membership to end, and we’ll help take care of the rest.

Group Training, Open Gym, and Dr. Dish reservations require at least 12 hours’ notice. Private Training requires at least 24 hours’ notice.

Late cancellations or missed sessions may result in the loss of the applicable session or credit.
