Membership

It depends on the plan. Weekly includes four Group Training sessions each billing cycle. Unlimited adds unlimited Group Training and Open Gym. Full Court adds Private Training and Dr. Dish sessions, and Family Unlimited covers two athletes. All plans include member pricing on select programs and services.

Yes — memberships are month-to-month with no long-term contract required.
