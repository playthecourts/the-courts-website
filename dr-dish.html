How It Works

Shoot. It’ll Get the Ball.

Pick your spots. Start the workout. Shoot.
Dr. Dish sends the ball back, keeps the reps moving, and gives athletes feedback while they work.

01

Pick Your Work.

Choose what you want to work on. Catch-and-shoot. Different spots. Shooting volume. Game-speed reps.

There are worse ways to spend 30 minutes.

02
Again.
Again.
Again.

Get the Reps.

The machine passes. You shoot.
Then it passes again.

And again. And again. And again.

03

Get the Feedback.

Makes and misses are only part of the story. Dr. Dish can track shot arc in real time, giving athletes another way to see what their shot is doing and adjust while they work.

Shoot. See. Adjust. Shoot again.

04

Come Back.

One workout tells you something. A bunch of workouts tell you a lot more. Saved performance data can help athletes see patterns, progress, and what still needs work.

Some days it's you vs. the machine.
Some days it's you vs. last Tuesday.
