Why Private Training?

Because Sometimes You Need Someone Watching You.

Group training gives athletes great reps. Private training gives a coach the chance to catch the little stuff.

The foot that lands wrong. The elbow that drifts. The platform angle. The extra step. The decision that comes half a second too late.

Little things.
Big difference.

Fix Themselves.

Meet Your Coaches

Someone's Actually Watching.

In the helpful way. Private training means a coach can see the detail, make the adjustment, watch the next rep, and adjust again.
Not just: "Do it again." More like: "Do it again. But this time, here's why."
