Participant Waiver & Release

Required before an athlete gets on the court.

This page is informational only. It does not itself serve as, or satisfy the requirement to complete, the participant waiver — viewing this page does not fulfill participation requirements. The waiver is affirmatively completed through the athlete registration / onboarding process.

Why It's Required

Basketball and volleyball are physical, competitive activities. Before an athlete participates in any Courts program — group training, private training, camps, leagues, or events — a parent or legal guardian must complete the applicable participation waiver and consent requirements on their behalf.

What It Generally Covers

Depending on the program, the waiver and related registration documents may address:

  • Assumption of risk associated with athletic participation
  • Liability release
  • Emergency contact information
  • Medical authorization, where appropriate
  • Code of conduct acknowledgment
  • Photo / video release or preference
Counsel review: this page describes the waiver at a high level only. The actual waiver and assumption-of-risk language must be professionally drafted and delivered through the registration/onboarding system, not published here.

Who Completes It

For minor athletes, the waiver must be completed by a parent or legal guardian — not the athlete.

When It's Completed

The waiver is completed as part of registering an athlete for a Courts program, before their first session.

Questions?

If you have questions about the waiver or your athlete's registration, contact us at hello@playthecourts.com or through our Contact page.
