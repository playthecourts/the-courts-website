Everyone Should Be Able to Get on the Court.

The Courts is committed to creating an inclusive experience, both in our facility and online.

We are working to make playthecourts.com accessible and usable for people of all abilities and will continue improving the site as our programs and digital experiences grow.

Our Accessibility Efforts

Our accessibility efforts include attention to areas such as:

  • Keyboard navigation
  • Screen-reader compatibility
  • Text and color contrast
  • Alternative text for meaningful images
  • Clear headings and page structure
  • Accessible forms and error messages
  • Captions or alternatives for meaningful video content

Need Help?

If you have difficulty accessing any part of our website, need information in another format, or need assistance registering for a Courts program, please reach out. We want to help.

hello@playthecourts.com

When contacting us, please let us know what you were trying to access and what would make it easier for you.
