Our Accessibility Efforts

We work to make our website and digital experiences easier to use and understand. Our accessibility efforts include attention to:

  • Keyboard navigation
  • Screen-reader compatibility
  • Readable text and color contrast
  • Alternative text for meaningful images
  • Clear headings and page structure
  • Accessible forms, labels, and error messages
  • Captions or other alternatives for meaningful video content
  • Touch targets and navigation that work across device sizes

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. As we update the website, add new tools, or connect third-party services, we'll continue working to improve the experience.

Some parts of the online experience may be provided through third-party services, such as scheduling, registration, account management, payments, maps, or other tools. While we do not control every aspect of those services, we want to help if you encounter an accessibility issue while using them.

Need Help?

If you have trouble accessing part of our website, need information in another format, or need help completing a registration or other online task, let us know. We'll do our best to help you access the information or service you need.

hello@playthecourts.com

When you contact us, it helps to tell us:

  • What page or feature you were trying to use
  • What problem you encountered
  • What format or type of help would make it easier

If something on the site is harder to use than it should be, we want to know.
