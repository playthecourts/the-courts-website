Accessibility Review

No formal accessibility audit or verification has been completed for playthecourts.com. Until one has, do not state that the site "complies with," "conforms to," or "is certified under" any specific WCAG level or accessibility standard — use commitment and ongoing-improvement language only, as below.

Our Accessibility Efforts

Our accessibility efforts include attention to:

  • Keyboard navigation
  • Screen-reader compatibility
  • Text and color contrast
  • Alternative text for meaningful images
  • Clear headings and page structure
  • Accessible forms and error messages
  • Captions or alternatives for meaningful video content

Need Help?

If you have trouble accessing part of our website, need information in another format, or need help registering for a Courts program, let us know. We want to make it easier.

hello@playthecourts.com

When contacting us, it helps to tell us what you were trying to access and what would make it easier for you.
