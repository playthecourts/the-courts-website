Coaching Is a Lot.

That's a Lot
of Tabs Open.

Coaching can be a weirdly solo job.

You're building the workouts. Texting the parents. Chasing gym space. Managing schedules. Answering "what time again?" for the fifth time. And somehow you're still expected to actually coach.

The Coach Collective gives independent basketball and volleyball coaches a place to train their athletes, run their program, and spend a little less time wondering where next Tuesday's workout is happening.

Maybe keep one tab open. We're not miracle workers.

The Idea

Your Program.
With a Home Court.

The Coach Collective is for independent coaches who already have something going.

One athlete. A handful of athletes. A growing training program.

You bring your people and your coaching. The Courts gives you a place to do the work.

It's Still Your Program.

Your Athletes.

You already did the hard part. Bring the people who trust you to coach them.

Your Program.

Run your workouts. Grow your groups. Coach your way.

Our Court.

A consistent place to train without making "find gym space" your second job.

Seems like you have enough jobs already.

Basketball

Bring the Workout.

Individual development. Small groups. Skill work. Your athletes. You coach. We'll provide the lines, hoops, and hardwood.

Volleyball

Bring the Group.

Passing. Setting. Attacking. Small-group development. You bring the reps. We'll provide the net.

The Collective Part

Good Coaches Make
Each Other Better, Too.

You're still running your own program. You're still coaching your own athletes.

But you're doing it around other people who care about the work as much as you do. Sometimes that's sharing an idea. Sometimes it's just somebody else who understands why you have 47 unread parent texts.
